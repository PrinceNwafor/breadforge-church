Our Story

We Are The Breadforge

An apostolic network in Ikorodu, Lagos, forging a generation of chosen ones — campus missionaries, Word-carriers, and flame-bearers for the end-time harvest.

Founded with Purpose

How It All Began

The Breadforge Church — formally known as the Eklektos Apostolic Network — was born out of a conviction: that God is calling out a chosen people (eklektos, Greek for "the elect") who will carry His apostolic fire into every campus, city, and nation.

Led by Pastor David Prince Ben from Ikorodu, Lagos, the church has grown from a small gathering of passionate believers into a growing apostolic community with a burning focus on campus ministry and raising the next generation of God's army.

The word "Breadforge" speaks to our core identity — we are a place where the Bread of Life (Jesus, the Word) is used to forge, shape, and equip ordinary people for extraordinary kingdom assignments.

What Drives Us

Mission, Vision & Values

🎯

Our Mission

To raise a holy, apostolic generation — forged in the Word of God — who will invade campuses, communities, and nations with the fire of the Holy Spirit.

🔭

Our Vision

A church that functions as a forge — where every believer is processed by the Word, set ablaze by the Spirit, and released as a weapon in the hands of God.

🔥

Our Values

Apostolic truth. Holy Spirit fire. Campus focus. Radical discipleship. Uncompromising Word. Community built on covenant.

A Man Forged in the Fire of God

Pastor David Prince Ben is the founder and Lead Pastor of The Breadforge Church (Eklektos Apostolic Network), based in Ikorodu, Lagos, Nigeria. He carries a burning apostolic mandate to raise an army of campus missionaries and spiritual soldiers for the end-time harvest.

Known for his fire-filled, deeply apostolic preaching, Pastor David Prince Ben operates with a distinct prophetic edge and an unwavering commitment to the authority of God's Word. His messages cut deep, build faith, and consistently direct believers toward their God-given purpose.

He has a particular heart for campuses — having received a divine mandate to focus the church's mission on university campuses, mobilising young men and women to become apostolic carriers of revival in their generation.

His ministry has taken him to various churches, campuses, and conferences, including gatherings with HWCN Ogbomoso and other apostolic networks across Nigeria, where his ministry consistently leaves a mark of spiritual weight and divine encounter.

"There is a different weight that comes when you sit under God's Word. That weight is what forges you for your destiny."

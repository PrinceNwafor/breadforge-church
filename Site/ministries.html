Where Do You Belong?

From campuses to families, from prayer to outreach — there is a place for every believer in the Breadforge family. Find your fire, discover your calling, and forge your destiny.

Ministry at the Breadforge is not a program — it's a calling. Our ministries exist to forge every believer into a weapon in God's hands, connected to a community of fire-filled, purpose-driven believers who are changing the world — starting with the campuses of Nigeria.

Your First Step

How to Get Plugged In

Finding your place in the Breadforge family is simple. Here's how we help every new person discover where they truly belong.

1
Visit on a Sunday

Come to a service and encounter the community. No pressure — just come and see.

2
Attend Newcomers Connect

Our monthly gathering is designed to help you find your fit. Meet Pastor David and the team, explore the ministries, ask questions.

3
Join a Home Group

Small groups are where deep community happens. We'll connect you with a group based on your location and life stage.

4
Step into Your Ministry

Every believer is called. We'll help you identify and activate the gifts God placed inside you — especially for campus missions.

🙋

Ready to Serve?

We have serving opportunities in every area — welcome team, campus outreach, prayer ministry, creative arts, and more.

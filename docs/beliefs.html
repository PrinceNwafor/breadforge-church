Doctrine & Conviction

Built on the Word.
Anchored in Truth.

We are an apostolic people shaped by Scripture from cover to cover. These are the convictions that frame everything we preach, everything we build, and everyone we send.

Our Foundation

The Bible Is Our Final Authority

We believe the Holy Bible — 66 books, Old and New Testaments — is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God. It is the supreme standard for all faith and practice. Every teaching, every program, and every decision at The Breadforge Church is measured against Scripture.

"All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training in righteousness, so that the servant of God may be thoroughly equipped for every good work."

2 Timothy 3:16–17

✝️

God: Father, Son & Holy Spirit

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three Persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. God is holy, sovereign, omnipotent, and intimately involved in the lives of His people. He is not distant; He speaks, He moves, and He still fills His church with His Spirit today.

Deuteronomy 6:4 · Matthew 28:19 · John 10:30

👑

Jesus Christ: Lord & Saviour

We believe Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man — born of a virgin, lived a sinless life, died on the cross as an atoning sacrifice for sin, rose bodily on the third day, ascended to the Father, and is coming again in glory. There is no other name by which men are saved.

John 1:1,14 · Isaiah 53 · Acts 4:12 · 1 Cor. 15:3–4

🕊️

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit is the third Person of the Trinity and is fully active in the church today. He convicts of sin, regenerates the soul, baptises believers with power, distributes spiritual gifts, and empowers the saints for every work of the Kingdom. We do not grieve or quench Him.

John 16:13 · Acts 1:8 · Acts 2:4 · 1 Cor. 12:4–11

♻️

Salvation by Grace Through Faith

We believe every person is born in sin and separated from God. Salvation is entirely by God's grace through faith in Jesus Christ — not by works, merit, or religious effort. The repentant heart that turns to Jesus is born again, forgiven, and made a new creation. Eternal life begins now.

Romans 3:23 · Ephesians 2:8–9 · John 3:3–7 · 2 Cor. 5:17

💧

Water Baptism & The Lord's Supper

We practise believer's baptism by immersion as a public declaration of faith and identification with the death, burial, and resurrection of Jesus Christ. We also observe the Lord's Supper as a regular act of remembrance and proclamation of Christ's sacrifice until He returns.

Matthew 28:19 · Romans 6:3–4 · 1 Cor. 11:23–26

🙌

Gifts of the Spirit

We believe all the gifts of the Holy Spirit — including tongues, prophecy, healing, and miracles — are available to the church in every generation. They are not relics of the apostolic age; they are tools God still uses to build His Kingdom, edify the body, and confirm the Word.

1 Cor. 12:7–11 · 1 Cor. 14:1 · Mark 16:17–18

⚔️

Spiritual Warfare & Authority

We believe believers are called to active spiritual warfare. Satan is a defeated enemy — defeated at the cross — and the church operates from a position of victory in Christ. We take authority in prayer, resist the enemy, and advance the Kingdom of God into every sphere of society.

Ephesians 6:10–18 · Luke 10:19 · Colossians 2:15

🌍

The Great Commission

We believe every believer is sent. The Great Commission is not optional and not limited to a professional clergy class — it is the mandate of the entire church. At The Breadforge, we take this seriously on campuses, in communities, and to the ends of the earth.

Matthew 28:18–20 · Mark 16:15 · Acts 1:8

🏛️

The Local Church

We believe the local church is God's primary vehicle for making disciples, equipping the saints, and advancing the Kingdom. It is not a building or a program — it is a covenant community. We are committed to genuine fellowship, accountability, discipleship, and apostolic governance.

Acts 2:42–47 · Eph. 4:11–13 · Hebrews 10:24–25

🌟

The Return of Christ & Eternity

We believe in the physical, bodily, and visible return of Jesus Christ. The dead will be raised, the living transformed, and every person will stand before God's judgement. Those who have trusted in Christ will spend eternity with Him. This hope shapes how we live and how we preach today.

1 Thessalonians 4:16–17 · John 5:28–29 · Revelation 20–22

Our Identity

What It Means to Be Apostolic

The word "apostolic" means "sent." An apostolic church is one that takes the missionary calling of the first church seriously — not as an occasional programme, but as the heartbeat of everything it does.

The early church in Acts was apostolic. They preached with power, moved in signs and wonders, planted churches, trained believers, and went to where people were. They didn't wait for the world to come to them.

At The Breadforge, we believe that same apostolic DNA is available today. We are not nostalgic for the early church — we are becoming it. Our campus missions mandate, our training culture, and our community outreach are all expressions of this apostolic identity.

Our Heart

A Forge Where Lives
Are Shaped for Eternity

The name says it all — we are a place where the Bread of Life shapes, refines, and forges ordinary people into carriers of extraordinary purpose.

Under the leadership of Pastor David Prince Ben, the Breadforge Church/Eklektos Apostolic Network exists to raise a holy, apostolic generation — especially on campuses — who carry the fire of God into every sphere of society.

First-Timer Guide

What to Expect
When You Arrive

We want your first visit to feel comfortable from the moment you step through the door. Here's a step-by-step of what a Sunday looks like.

1
You'll be warmly welcomed

Our welcome team greets everyone at the door. There's no awkward first-timer badge — just a warm handshake and a seat.

2
Worship opens the service

Expect Spirit-led worship — real, deep, and intentional. Not performance. We make room for God's presence before anything else.

3
The Word is preached with fire

Pastor David Prince Ben opens the Scripture with authority and clarity. Come with your Bible — bring a notebook if you can.

4
Ministry time & connection

After the service, the team stays around to connect, pray with anyone who needs it, and help you find your next step.

Practical Info

What to Know Before You Come

👗

Dress Code

Come as you are. We don't have a dress code. Casual, smart, or formal — what matters is your heart, not your wardrobe.

📚

What to Bring

Bring your Bible if you have one. Bring a notebook — the preaching is rich and worth writing down. Bring an open heart.

👶

Children

Children are welcome in the main service. We have a children's program on Sundays — ask at the welcome desk when you arrive.

🅿️

Parking & Access

We're located in Ikorodu, Lagos. Contact us ahead of your visit for exact directions and parking details for the area.

⏱️

How Long Is It?

Sunday services run about 90 minutes to 2 hours. We never rush God's presence — but we're also mindful of your time.

🤝

First-Timer Reception

On select Sundays we host a brief first-timer meet with Pastor David. Let us know you're coming and we'll make sure you're included.
